About

DocuBiz is a student-developed system that integrates core business processes to improve worlkflows and improve convenience.

We aim to provide a role-based approach to enhance the efficiency, accessibility and organization of business processes on a single web-based platform.

We seek to increase data accessibility, decrease manual work and make basic administrative tasks more organized. Overall, the system aims to offer a useful web-based solution that can help administrators and staff manage important business operations on a single platform.
